Part 2 – Question-Response

Luyện thi TOEIC · Hỏi – Đáp

🎯 25 câu luyện tập · có giải thích tiếng Việt
🎯 Mục lục TOEIC🏠 Trang chủ ✍️ Làm bài tập

📌 Cách làm Part 2

Nghe từ để hỏi đầu câu: Who / What / When / Where / Why / How / Do-Does / Is-Are …
  • When → thời gian (next Monday, at 3 o'clock) · Where → nơi chốn (in the lobby, on the desk) · Who → người, chức danh, phòng ban
  • Đáp án lặp lại đúng từ trong câu hỏi hoặc từ phát âm gần giống thường là bẫy (copy – coffee, meeting – meet)
  • Câu trả lời gián tiếp vẫn đúng: "Shall we start?" → "We're still waiting for Mr. Lee." (chưa bắt đầu được)
  • Câu hỏi Yes/No có thể được trả lời mà không có Yes/No: "Have you finished the report?" → "I'll send it this afternoon."
Where is the meeting being held? → In conference room B.
🇻🇳 Cuộc họp được tổ chức ở đâu? → Ở phòng họp B.

✍️ Bài tập

🎧 Bấm 🔊 Nghe để nghe (giọng đọc của trình duyệt, nghe được khi không có mạng nếu máy có sẵn giọng tiếng Anh). Part 2 chỉ nghe, không in sẵn đáp án — đáp án và lời thoại hiện ra sau khi chọn.

1.
🗣️ When will the new printer be delivered?
(A) Yes, it prints in color.
(B) In the supply room.
(C) Sometime on Thursday morning.
✅ (C) Sometime on Thursday morning. — Hỏi "When" → cần thời gian. "In the supply room" trả lời Where; câu C bẫy từ "printer – prints".
2.
🗣️ Where should I put these boxes?
(A) Yes, I put it there.
(B) About twenty of them.
(C) Just leave them by the door.
✅ (C) Just leave them by the door. — Hỏi "Where" → nơi chốn: "by the door". Câu B trả lời How many.
3.
🗣️ Who is in charge of the marketing budget?
(A) At the end of the month.
(B) Ms. Patel handles that.
(C) It's too expensive.
✅ (B) Ms. Patel handles that. — Hỏi "Who" → người: Ms. Patel. Câu B bẫy chủ đề tiền; câu C trả lời When.
4.
🗣️ Why was the flight delayed?
(A) Because of the heavy snow.
(B) I'd prefer a window seat.
(C) At gate twelve.
✅ (A) Because of the heavy snow. — Hỏi "Why" → lý do: "Because of…".
5.
🗣️ How often do you check your e-mail?
(A) By train, usually.
(B) It's a new e-mail address.
(C) Several times a day.
✅ (C) Several times a day. — "How often" hỏi tần suất → "several times a day". "By train" trả lời How (phương tiện).
6.
🗣️ Would you like some coffee?
(A) No thanks, I just had some.
(B) The copy machine is broken.
(C) He likes his job.
✅ (A) No thanks, I just had some. — Lời mời "Would you like…?" → nhận/từ chối. Câu C bẫy âm "coffee – copy".
7.
🗣️ Have you finished the sales report yet?
(A) Sales went up last quarter.
(B) I'll send it to you this afternoon.
(C) Yes, the store is open.
✅ (B) I'll send it to you this afternoon. — Trả lời gián tiếp: chưa xong, chiều nay sẽ gửi. Câu B lặp từ "sales".
8.
🗣️ Can you help me move this desk?
(A) Sure, where do you want it?
(B) It's a wooden desk.
(C) I moved here last year.
✅ (A) Sure, where do you want it? — Lời nhờ "Can you…?" → đồng ý/từ chối. Câu C bẫy "move – moved".
9.
🗣️ Which bus goes to the airport?
(A) The number 15 does.
(B) It takes about an hour.
(C) I'll pick you up.
✅ (A) The number 15 does. — "Which bus" → xác định chiếc xe: "The number 15".
10.
🗣️ Isn't the store open on Sundays?
(A) Only in December.
(B) I'll store them in the cabinet.
(C) Yes, it's a large store.
✅ (A) Only in December. — Câu hỏi phủ định: chỉ mở Chủ nhật vào tháng 12. Câu B bẫy "store" (cất giữ).
11.
🗣️ Shall we start the meeting now?
(A) It started raining.
(B) In the main hall.
(C) We're still waiting for Mr. Lee.
✅ (C) We're still waiting for Mr. Lee. — Trả lời gián tiếp: chưa bắt đầu được vì còn chờ ông Lee.
12.
🗣️ How much does it cost to ship this package?
(A) About twenty dollars.
(B) By express mail.
(C) It's a large ship.
✅ (A) About twenty dollars. — "How much" → giá tiền. Câu C bẫy "ship" (con tàu).
13.
🗣️ Whose jacket is this?
(A) I think it's Tom's.
(B) Yes, it's on the chair.
(C) It's very warm outside.
✅ (A) I think it's Tom's. — "Whose" hỏi chủ sở hữu → "Tom's".
14.
🗣️ What time does the training session begin?
(A) Two new trainees.
(B) In the training room.
(C) At nine thirty sharp.
✅ (C) At nine thirty sharp. — "What time" → giờ cụ thể.
15.
🗣️ Do you know where the nearest bank is?
(A) I don't have an account.
(B) At ten o'clock.
(C) There's one across the street.
✅ (C) There's one across the street. — Câu hỏi gián tiếp về nơi chốn → "across the street".
16.
🗣️ The air conditioner isn't working.
(A) Yes, I'll air the room.
(B) I'll call maintenance right away.
(C) It's working hours.
✅ (B) I'll call maintenance right away. — Câu trần thuật báo vấn đề → phản ứng xử lý. Câu B/C bẫy "working", "air".
17.
🗣️ Should we take a taxi or walk to the restaurant?
(A) Let's walk, it's not far.
(B) I ate there yesterday.
(C) Yes, a table for four.
✅ (A) Let's walk, it's not far. — Câu hỏi lựa chọn "A or B" → chọn một: walk.
18.
🗣️ Why don't we order lunch for the team?
(A) At about noon.
(B) That's a great idea.
(C) Because the order was late.
✅ (B) That's a great idea. — "Why don't we…?" là lời đề nghị, không hỏi lý do → đồng ý.
19.
🗣️ Who's going to replace Ms. Chen while she's on leave?
(A) It's a nice place.
(B) She left early today.
(C) Mr. Ito from accounting.
✅ (C) Mr. Ito from accounting. — Hỏi người thay thế → Mr. Ito. Câu C bẫy "replace – place".
20.
🗣️ How long have you worked here?
(A) It's a long way.
(B) Every weekday.
(C) For about five years.
✅ (C) For about five years. — "How long" + hiện tại hoàn thành → khoảng thời gian "for five years".
21.
🗣️ Where can I find the user manual?
(A) It's very useful.
(B) It should be in the top drawer.
(C) Yes, I can find it.
✅ (B) It should be in the top drawer. — Hỏi nơi chốn → "in the top drawer".
22.
🗣️ Are you attending the conference next week?
(A) I haven't decided yet.
(B) It was very interesting.
(C) The conference room is booked.
✅ (A) I haven't decided yet. — Trả lời chưa quyết định là hợp lý. Câu B sai thì (quá khứ).
23.
🗣️ When is the deadline for the proposal?
(A) It's due this Friday.
(B) I proposed a new plan.
(C) In the director's office.
✅ (A) It's due this Friday. — "When … deadline" → "due this Friday". Câu B bẫy "proposal – proposed".
24.
🗣️ Could you send me a copy of the contract?
(A) I'd like a cup of coffee.
(B) The contract was signed.
(C) I'll e-mail it to you now.
✅ (C) I'll e-mail it to you now. — Lời nhờ gửi tài liệu → "I'll e-mail it now".
25.
🗣️ What did you think of the presentation?
(A) At two o'clock.
(B) It was very informative.
(C) I'll present it tomorrow.
✅ (B) It was very informative. — Hỏi ý kiến → nhận xét "very informative".
